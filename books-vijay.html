Vijay Book

My latest book focuses on Vijay, the Chief Minister of Tamil Nadu. The book presents my perspective on Vijay, his political journey, leadership, and the broader vision explored in the book.

It also presents my perspective on the concept of starting a political party and governing or ruling 97 countries around the world — reflecting my broader interest in exploring ambitious ideas about leadership, governance, society, and the future through books and research.

Vijay Book is available in 50 languages worldwide, making it accessible to readers across different countries and linguistic communities.
